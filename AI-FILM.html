🔗 6 merge methods — cut gayab karne ke liye

    

      
1. MovementWo frame right se nikli → agla shot me bhi right ja rahi hai

      
2. ActionHaath parde ki taraf badha → agla shot me wahi haath parde ko chhu raha hai

      
3. EyelineUpar-baayein dekha → agla shot wahi upar-baayein, usi height se

      
4. ShapeOdhni ka curve → agla shot patton ka wahi curve

      
5. ColourUske kapde ka hara → agla shot poora hara jungle

      
6. ConsequenceKangan giraft se gir gayi → agla shot zameen par wahi kangan, wo ja chuki

    

    
#6 sabse strong hai. Poori film me kam se kam 2 baar lagao.

  


  

    
📋 Post se pehle checklist

    

      
⬜Har shot me chehra sahi umar ka — jawaan, modern

      
⬜Har video prompt me short lock, poori DNA nahi

      
⬜Har clip me beat timeline — har second bhara

      
⬜Har face shot me turn (start → end state) + ek tell

      
⬜Har travelling shot me kuch peeche chhootta hai

      
⬜Har detail shot pichhle action ka nateeja

      
⬜Poori film me ek bold move + ek bada effect

      
⬜Koi prompt me "no" nahi · naam nahi · weathered nahi

      
⬜Film 4+ jagah travel · koi jagah 3 shot se zyada nahi

      
⬜Har clip ka pehla 1 second kaat diya

      
⬜Saare clips ka AI audio mute · gaana app library se

      
⬜Mute test — bina awaaz story samajh aati hai?

      
⬜Peak se 1.5-2 ghante pehle upload
