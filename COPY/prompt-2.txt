RULE 2 — THE AGE LINE  ⭐ v9 (character budha nahi lagega)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
EVERY character line starts with the age. This is the single thing that
stops a twenty-year-old from rendering as a forty-five-year-old.

Write it like: "a young woman of about nineteen, smooth unlined skin,
full cheeks, bright clear eyes"

⭐ BANNED for anyone under thirty, in every prompt:
   wrinkled · lined · weathered · aged · gaunt · hollow ·
   leathery · hardened features · mature
⭐ BANNED for their clothing and ornaments:
   tarnished · worn · frayed · faded · dusty · threadbare · patched
⭐ BANNED for the look of the film:
   vintage · period film · archival · old print · faded colours ·
   sepia · 35mm film grain · muted palette

The world is old. The FILM is not. A god, a village, a hundred years
ago — none of that is an instruction to make the picture look old or
faded. Old is set dressing, not image quality.

RULE 3 — MODERN, CLEAN, EXPENSIVE (the visual target)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
The film must look like it was shot THIS YEAR on a high-end digital
cinema camera. Write it this way:

  Photographed on a modern large-format digital cinema camera,
  50mm prime lens at T1.8, shallow depth of field, clean sharp image,
  no grain, deep true blacks, clean bright highlights held in check,
  strong contrast, healthy warm skin with fine natural texture, a soft
  sheen on the cheekbone, rich but restrained colour.

⭐ COLOUR: rich, not faded. Greens must be green, gold must be gold,
   red must be red. Only ONE colour may be loud per frame.
   Faded is not the same as tasteful. Faded reads as cheap.
⭐ LIGHT: one clear source, its direction, and a real shadow on one
   side of the face. Light coming from everywhere is the biggest
   computer-render tell there is.
⭐ Never light a face only from behind — backlight hides skin texture,
   and hidden texture is what reads as plastic.

RULE 4 — DIVINITY, WITHOUT GLOW
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
If the story has gods or divine figures, divinity comes from what they

RULE 7 — ✨ THE IMPERFECTIONS BLOCK (this is what makes it real)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Every image prompt ends with this block, worded positively:

  healthy skin with fine natural texture, slight natural unevenness in
  the skin colour, a soft sheen on the cheeks and the bridge of the
  nose, a few loose strands of hair out of place, fabric with real
  weight sitting in real folds, small cast shadows under the jaw and
  the collarbone, natural asymmetry between the two sides of the face

Perfection is the tell. A face with nothing out of place reads as a
render. One loose strand of hair does more for realism than any
resolution.
