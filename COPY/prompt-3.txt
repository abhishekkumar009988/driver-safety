RULE 3 — ACTION-ATTACH LAW  ⭐ v9 ("action attach nahi lag raha" ka fix)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
The reason clips look like separate singles is that every clip started
and ended at rest. This is the fix. It is the most important rule here.

  1. NO SHOT STARTS AT REST and NO SHOT ENDS AT REST, except the very
     first and the very last shot of the film.
  2. The LAST 0.5 second of every video prompt must describe a movement
     that is still going when the clip ends. Write it in present tense:
     "she is still turning as the shot ends".
  3. The FIRST 0.5 second of the next video prompt must complete that
     same movement, from the same body position, same hand, same side.
  4. Write the position in words, both times, and make them identical:
     "right hand at chest height, elbow bent, weight on the left foot".
  5. Moving camera continues: if a shot ends mid-pan, the next shot
     starts mid-pan in the same direction, same speed.
  6. Eyeline carries: whoever was looking left at the end, the next shot
     must cut to what was on their left — and that person is looking
     back, one beat late.

If two consecutive cards cannot be joined this way, the shot list is
wrong. Fix the shot list, not the cards.

RULE 4 — 4–6 CAMERA SETUPS, ALL ANGLES VERIFIED  ⭐ v9
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Choose 4 to 6 setups for the whole film and never invent a seventh.

  A — WIDE / master: the whole place, tiny people, one landmark visible
  B — MEDIUM: waist-up, two people if they are together
  C — CLOSE: face, or hands doing the important thing
  D — OVER-SHOULDER: we look past one person at the other
  E — HIGH or TOP-DOWN: the ground, the spread, the arrangement
  F — LOW: from knee height looking up, for power moments only

Camera height must be written in every card, in centimetres:
  ground 0 · knee 45 · chest 120 · eye 160 · high 250 · top 300+

Laws:
  · Every scene must be covered from at least FOUR of the setups.
  · Never two shots in a row from the same setup, unless the second is a
    deliberate hold on the same face.
  · Exactly ONE bold camera move in the entire film (a fast push, a
    whip, a rise). Everything else is a slow drift, a slow push or a
    locked frame. Write which shot has the bold move, and mark it.
  · After a bold move, the next shot is locked or nearly locked.
  · I am checking for this myself. If a setup is used fewer than four
    times, or a scene sits in one setup, say so and fix it before
    sending.

RULE 5 — HOLD, DRAPE AND WEAR MECHANICS  ⭐ v9
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
This is where AI clips fall apart. Never write "they embrace" or
"she adjusts her dupatta". Write the physical steps, in order, with
the limbs named.

  HOLDING:   which hand, where it grips, how hard, and what the other
             hand does at the same time.
             "Her left hand closes on his right forearm just below the
             elbow, thumb on the outside; her right hand stays at her
             side."
  DRAPING:   lift → travel → land. Three steps, three timestamps.
             "She lifts the cloth with her right hand to shoulder
             height, carries it across her chest, and lays it over the
             left shoulder; it settles and stays."
  WEARING:   what moves when the body moves. Sleeve, hem, veil, hair.
             "When she turns, the hem sweeps a quarter turn behind her
             and settles."
  TOUCHING:  contact must be visible for at least one full second, and
             the second person must react to it — a breath, a blink, a
             small change of weight.

Never let a cloth, a hand or a garland pass through a body, and never
let it change from one shoulder to the other between shots.

RULE 6 — MID-SCENE MERGE SHOTS  ⭐ v9 ("beech beech ke seenshots")
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Between two scenes, and between two big emotions inside one scene,
insert ONE short shot, about 4 to 6 seconds, that belongs to both
sides. It must contain two of these at the same time:

  · the same movement continuing (a turn that becomes another turn)
  · the same shape (a circle, a line, a curve repeating)
  · the same colour passing from one object to another
  · the same action done by two different people
  · the same eyeline handed over
  · the same emotion changing owner

Say in the card: JOINS Scene 2 → Scene 3. Name which two of the six
devices it uses. If it does not join two things, it is not a merge
shot — cut it.

RULE 7 — COSTUME IS AN ACTOR
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RULE 9 — EMOTION PHASE SWITCHES THE LOOK  ⭐ v9
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
The film has phases (for example: peace → want → test → break → grace).
Every card states its phase, and every phase gets its own look, chosen
automatically by the emotion — I do not want to decide it:

  peace:  wide, soft light, greens and cream, slow drift, no VFX
  want:   closer, warmer, one loud colour, light from one side, small
          natural VFX already building (dust, drifting petals, heat)
  test:   tighter, cooler, harder shadow, faster drift, weather VFX
  break:  closest, darkest, hand-held, one big VFX event lands HERE
  grace:  opens wide again, bright clean light, the loudest colour of
          the film, calm locked frame

Write the phase on every card and keep the look consistent inside a
phase, so the change between phases is felt as a change in the film.

RULE 10 — VEO GRAMMAR (this is how the video prompt gets written)

RULE 12 — ATMOSPHERE NUMBER SYSTEM  ⭐ v9 (smoke · fog · dust · rain)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
"Some smoke" and "light fog" mean NOTHING to a video model. It guesses,
and the guess is always too much smoke, too weak a face, and a new face
invented behind the smoke. So every effect gets a NUMBER, and the number
means exactly the same thing in every shot of this film.

⭐ SMOKE LEVEL — write the number, then the look, then the direction

 0 — no smoke at all.
 1 — one wisp thinner than a wrist, one tenth of the frame at most.
     The face is completely clean and sharp. Safe for every face shot.
 2 — two or three wisps at chest height, drifting at walking pace.
     The face stays clean; only the outline of the shoulders softens.
 3 — a low band up to the waist, plus two moving columns. Eyes and mouth
     staying sharp; the chin edge may soften. THIS IS THE HEAVIEST SMOKE
     ALLOWED IN ANY SHOT THAT CONTAINS A READABLE FACE.
 4 — smoke up to the neck, columns crossing the face. Eyes and brow
     only. Never in a dialogue shot, never in a face close-up.
 5 — the whole frame filled, the person is a silhouette. No face detail
     of any kind.

⭐ FOG LEVEL — the number is the height of the fog ON THE BODY

 0 — none; ground and distance readable.
 1 — ankle height; the feet disappear, the ground still readable.
 2 — knee height; the legs vanish, the body fully clear.
 3 — waist height; faces in the middle distance start to go soft. Any
     face shot in fog must stay at 0, 1 or 2.
 4 — chest height; the person's own face goes soft. Silhouettes and
     wides only.
 5 — full frame; no ground, no distance, no face. A dreamy or lost
     moment only, never a plot action.

⭐ DUST / HAZE 1 motes in a beam · 2 dust off the ground under
   footsteps · 3 a beam thick enough to see the light itself · 4 the air
   greys out and the distance is lost.

⭐ RAIN 1 a few slow drops on skin, visible only against dark cloth ·
   2 steady drops, visible against dark backgrounds · 3 heavy; water
   standing on the ground, splash at the feet · 4 downpour; faces not
   usable.

⭐ FIRE / FLAME — write the count, the height in centimetres, and where
   each one is: "two oil lamps, flame six centimetres, one left of frame
   at chest height, one behind her at knee height."

⭐ Every effect needs a SOURCE that is in the frame, or was in the frame
   a moment ago: the fire that makes the smoke, the window that makes
   the beam, the cloud that makes the rain, the breath that makes the
   steam. Effect with no source reads as a computer mistake.

RULE 13 — FACE REVEAL LAW  ⭐ v9 (chehra kab dikhega, kitna dikhega)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
A face hidden behind smoke is where identity dies: the model invents new
features every time it has to guess. So:

 1. Any shot where the audience must READ the face: smoke 0-2, fog 0-2,
    rain 0-2, dust 0-2. Write the numbers.
 2. The heavy levels (smoke 4-5, fog 4-5, rain 4) are for silhouettes,
    wides and object shots ONLY.
 3. A REVEAL is written as a MOVEMENT, with a direction and a duration:
    "the smoke column drifts to the left and clears her eyes and mouth
    within two seconds, and stays clear for the rest of the shot."
    Never write "the smoke clears" with no direction and no time.
 4. The reveal shot gets ONE light source at forty-five degrees from the
    front, with its distance written: "a warm oil lamp, chest height,
    left of camera, one and a half metres away." A face revealed
    against the light stays a shadow, and the model fills that shadow
    with a different face.
 5. AFTER a reveal, the next two shots stay at smoke 0-1, so the face is
    locked in the audience's memory. Never reveal and re-cover at once.
 6. The level may change by ONE step between two shots, unless something
    inside the shot causes the change — and then write the cause: the
    door opens, the fire is thrown, the wind turns.

RULE 15 — THE TRACE LAW  ⭐ v9 (story kuch, image kuch — ye band)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Every card must be traceable back to my words. If it cannot be traced,
it came from you, not from me — and that is the whole problem.

  1. Every card opens with STORY LINE: a short quote from MY STORY or

  2. If a card has no line, it does not exist. Either delete it, or mark

  4. Never upgrade my words. If I wrote "she looks at him", do not write

     Anything marked invented must be justified in one line, or cut.

RULE 16 — THE STILL IS THE TRUTH  ⭐ v9 (video naya scene kyun banata hai)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Three different things were being described three ways: the story, the
image, and the video. That is why the film never matches. This rule makes
them one chain:

   STORY LINE  →  IMAGE PROMPT  →  VIDEO PROMPT
   (my words)     (the still)       (motion only)

  1. The IMAGE is generated from the story line plus the DNA blocks.
     Nothing else goes into the image prompt.
  2. The VIDEO asks for ONE thing only: movement. It must not describe
     the place, the clothes, the face, the light or the colour again.
     Those are in the still, and the model copies what it sees.
  3. Every video prompt opens with these two lines, copied exactly:
       "The uploaded still is the truth. Keep the face, clothes,
        colours, light, background, number of people and position in
        frame exactly as they are."
       "Only these movements change:"
  4. Then the timestamps. Then the sound sentence. Then the negative
     list. Nothing else.
  5. If the video prompt would need a new object, a new place or a new
     person, then the STILL is wrong — go back and fix the still. Never
     fix it inside the video prompt.
  6. The camera move belongs to the video prompt, but it is written on
     the card once, and it must be the same move the still was framed
     for.
  7. First frame, last frame: when the tool offers it, the first frame is
     the still, and the last frame is described in one plain sentence on
     the card, so Extend continues from a known picture instead of a
     guess.

RULE 17 — NOTHING ADDED  ⭐ v9 (apni marzi se cheezein jodna band)

What is not in my story, my STORY LOCK or my inventory does not go into

Birth, death, wedding, farewell and other ritual steps: use only the

If something is genuinely missing and the shot cannot be built without it,
