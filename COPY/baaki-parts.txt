===== ⭐ v9 ME KYA BADLA (tumhari bataayi hui galtiyan) =====
| # | Tumhari galti | v9 me fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Acting nakli — chehra patthar jaisa | **PERFORMANCE ENGINE** — thought → eye → arrival → control → leak. Purani "face holds exactly as it is" line **poori tarah hata di** |
| 2 | Character budha, modern nahi | **AGE LINE** — DNA umar se shuru. 30 se kam par "wrinkled/weathered/aged" ban |
| 3 | VFX kuch nahi | **VFX = story emotion se**. Chhota natural event har shot, 1 bada event peak par |
| 4 | Story meri jaisi nahi banti | **MY THINGS FIRST** — tumhari har cheez film me aayegi. AI badal nahi sakta |
| 5 | Look pehle, story baad me | **STORY FIRST, LOOK SECOND** — pehle emotional flow, usse location/light/colour/VFX nikalta hai |
| 6 | Do log ek frame me, koi rishta nahi | **RELATIONSHIP ENGINE** — intention → action → reaction → consequence. Reaction **late** aati hai |
| 7 | Kapda static lock tha | **COSTUME ACTION CONTINUITY** — kapda actor hai, lock nahi |
| 8 | Detail shots bekaar | **DETAIL = pichhle shot ka result + agle ka setup.** Khaali pretty frame reject |
| 9 | Merge sirf object se | **6 MERGE TYPES** — movement · shape · colour · action · eyeline · emotion |
| 10 | Ek jagah ghoomti rehti hai | **PROGRESS LAW** — har 2 shot me naya background, landmark bada hota aaye |
| 11 | Face change | **DNA LINE** har prompt me, word-for-word |
| 12 | Cartoon / calendar-art | Naam ban · glow words ban · portrait rule · realism block **shuru me** |
| 13 | **2nd prompt me hi video prompt de deta hai** | **STEP LOCK** — P2 sirf portrait banata hai. Video prompt ya camera move aaya to poora jawab reject |
| 14 | Smoke/fog ka amount koi nahi batata, face dhuyen me gayab | **ATMOSPHERE NUMBER SYSTEM** — smoke 0–5, fog 0–5. Level likha jaata hai, andaza nahi |
| 15 | "Thoda fog" likha, model ne poora frame bhar diya | **FACE REVEAL LAW** — face ke shot me level 2 se zyada kabhi nahi. Reveal = movement + direction + 2 second |
| 16 | Frame me kya-kya hai, ginti nahi | **FRAME INVENTORY** — har card par count: kitne log, kitni light, kitni cheezein, kitne metre |
| 17 | **Story kuch aur, image kuch aur** | **TRACE LAW** — har card ke sabse upar STORY LINE, jis line se wo shot bana hai. Bina line = card delete |
| 18 | **Video me naya scene ban jaata hai** | **STILL IS THE TRUTH** — video prompt sirf *movement* likhta hai. Dikhaawat dobara describe nahi hoti |
| 19 | AI apni marzi se cheezein jod deta hai | **NOTHING ADDED** — jo tumne nahi bola wo frame me nahi. Kuch chahiye to ASSUMPTIONS me likho, banane se pehle |
