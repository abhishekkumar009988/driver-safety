  ⭐ WHEN TO PIN A LAST FRAME — AND WHEN IT RUINS THE CLIP
  Many tools accept a first frame AND a last frame. Used correctly it
  is the strongest continuity tool there is. Used wrongly it is the
  reason a clip suddenly CUTS to a different scene halfway through.

  ⚠️ THE RULE: pin a last frame ONLY when the two stills are the same
  place, the same framing class and only a small change apart.

  A video model treats the two pinned frames as one continuous motion
  path. If they are far apart — different location, wide to extreme
  close-up, subject on the left then on the right — the model cannot
  travel between them in four seconds, so it does the only thing it
  can: it invents a camera move that does not exist, or it dissolves
  and morphs one scene into the other. On screen that reads as the
  clip changing scene in the middle. That is not the tool failing.
  That is two endpoints too far apart.

  ✅ PIN THE LAST FRAME when:
     • both stills are in the SAME location
     • the shot size changes by at most one step (medium → medium
       close, never wide → extreme close-up)
     • the subject stays on roughly the same side of the frame
     • the light direction and colour are identical
     • the whole change is something a body could really do in the
       clip's length

  ❌ DO NOT PIN A LAST FRAME when:
     • the next shot is in a DIFFERENT PLACE. This is the big one.
       Never pin across a location change. Let the clip end on its
       own end state and make a normal cut in the edit.
     • the framing jumps more than one step
     • the subject crosses to the other side of the frame
     • the light changes

     BLOCK 3, the end state, with real care.

━━━ RULES FOR THE CARDS ━━━
- One clip = ONE action. If a description needs "and" between two
  physical actions, split it into two shots. A clip asked to do
  three things will skip two.
- Clip length: 3-4s for a look, a blink, a turn, a reaction ·
  5s for walking or reaching · 8s for approaching or a full beat.
  Shorter clips drift less. Reactions are best at 3-4s.
- CHAIN inside a continuous moment. CUT only for a new time or
  place. Say which on every card.
- Body state at the end of a shot must equal the body state at the
  start of the next. If they differ, the change must be shown —
  that is a missing shot, add it to the chain and renumber.
- Nothing held may vanish or swap hands unexplained.
- No dialogue. The film must work on mute. Give ambient sound per
  shot instead.
- Everything must be supported by the research and the chain.
- EVERY CARD CARRIES ITS JOIN. On every card, under the header,
  write two lines:
     JOINS FROM: what was moving at the end of the previous shot,
     and how this shot picks that same movement up.
     HANDS TO: what is still moving as this shot ends, so the next
     shot can catch it.
  Shot 01 writes "JOINS FROM: film opens" and still writes HANDS TO.
- NO SHOT MAY START FROM STILL unless the previous shot ended with
  that same body already still. Movement at the cut is what makes
  two clips read as one continuous piece.
- A NEW PLACE IS ALWAYS ENTERED ON SCREEN. If this card is the first
  in a new location, the character must physically come into it in
  this shot or the one before. Never a waiting frame.
- THRESHOLD SHOTS ARE REAL SHOTS. Doors, gates, steps, paths,
  corners get their own card with their own image and video prompt.
  They are not optional and they are not filler — they are the
  glue. A person must be doing something physical in every one.
- EVERY SHOT CARRIES ITS PHASE from ㉘ and its numbers from ⑲.
  A card with no phase, or with "some smoke" instead of a level,
  is an unfinished card.

    card that is NOT in my story or the STORY LOCK? Cut it, or move
    it into MY ASSUMPTIONS.
  • Is MY ASSUMPTIONS printed at the very end, one line per choice?

PASS 2 — THE MOTION HANDOVER
  For each pair, put shot N's closing motion beside shot N+1's
  opening motion. Not merely compatible — the SAME movement from
  two angles. Any pair where it is not is a defect.

PASS 2B — THE JOINS AND THE THRESHOLDS
  Walk the film as a route, out loud, in one sentence:
  "she is HERE, then she goes THROUGH here, then she is HERE."
  If you cannot say it without the word "then suddenly", the film is
  not connected. Defects: any new location not physically ENTERED on
  screen · any place left without seeing her begin to leave · any
  A→B move with no threshold shot of a real physical action · any
  join row with fewer than two things carrying across · any
  threshold that is scenery with nobody in it.
  Fix by writing the missing threshold shot and renumbering.
  ⭐ And between two SCENES: is the merge shot from ㉓ there, with
  its two named devices?

PASS 2C — THE OPENING
  Look only at shot 01 and shot 02. Is something already moving at
  0.0s, or does the film warm up? Does shot 02 physically grow out
  of shot 01? A slow establishing wide, an empty landscape, a fade
  in, or a static frame waiting for someone to enter is a defect.

PASS 2D — HANDOFFS AND SAFE SHOTS
  • Read the SHOT TYPE column down. Count wide and full-body rows
    against detail rows (hands, eyes, objects, silhouettes, feet,
    backs of heads). If detail shots are not roughly three to one,
    rewrite until they are. This one ratio decides whether the film
    reads as expensive or as AI.
  • Is there at least one full silhouette, on a big moment?
  • Are there at least TWO object handoffs, one on the hardest jump
    in place or time? For each, do all four things match across the
    cut — the object, the direction, the speed, the position in
    frame? Is the handoff object already in the story?
  • Is any full-body walking shot longer than two seconds? Cut it
    down or replace it with feet, or with a silhouette.

PASS 3 — THE ACTING
  Does every face shot state what the character wants, what the face
  shows, what it hides, and one tell? Is the expression written as
  muscles at full strength, so the still already carries it? Any
  named emotion left standing alone is a defect.

PASS 3A — THE PERFORMANCE SWEEP
  Every shot with a face, check all six:
  • Does the face CHANGE during the clip, with a named second where
    it turns? One held expression across a whole clip is the single
    biggest reason acting reads as AI.
  • Is there a THOUGHT written in the character's own words?
  • Is there exactly one LEAK? Not three.
  • Are the hands and body doing something while the face feels?
  • Is the big emotion HELD DOWN rather than performed?
  • Does any video prompt still say the face holds exactly as it is
    in the uploaded frame? Delete that wording now.

PASS 3B — THE CALENDAR ART SWEEP
  • Does every image prompt OPEN with the capture — camera, lens,
    aperture, shot size, camera height? If the realism wording is
    parked at the end, move it to the front.
  • Is the word "photorealistic" used as a magic word? Delete it.
  • Is there ONE named light source with a direction, a bright side
    AND a shadow side? Is anyone lit only from behind and still
    half-visible? Either add a named front fill, or push it all the
    way to a full black silhouette.
  • Does the film have ONE full silhouette shot on a big moment?
  • Search every prompt and delete: glowing, luminous, radiant,
    aura, halo, god rays, light shafts, sunbeams, golden mist,
    golden haze, divine light, ethereal, dreamlike, otherworldly,
    magical, shimmering, sparkling, floating petals, floating
    particles, bloom, lens flare, backlit glow, rim glow, heavenly.
  • Divine characters: is the divinity carried by MATERIALS (heavy
    silk, worked gold, uncut gems, fresh flowers) and not by light?
  • Is the TEXTURE there — fine skin texture, a sheen on the
    cheekbone, separate hair strands, real fabric?
  • ⚠️ Is any AGEING language there — pores, wrinkles, weathered,
    tarnish, faded, worn, slub, film grain, muted? On a young
    character every one of those is a defect.
  • ⚠️⚠️ Is there a BAN LIST in any prompt? Search for "no ", "not ",
    "without", "avoid". Every one is a defect. Rewrite each as a
    positive fact. The only exception is a real separate negative
    box, no more than six terms, in that box only.
  • ⚠️ Does any prompt contain a DEITY'S NAME — Krishna, Radha,
    Shiva, Ram, Hanuman, Durga — or Lord, Shri, deity, divine, God?
    Delete every one and write the physical description instead.
  • Any poster buzzword — 8K, 4K, ultra-realistic, masterpiece,
    premium, devotional, majestic, epic, breathtaking, cinematic,
    poster, artwork, rendering? Delete and put a lens or a light in
    its place.
  • Is the colour restrained, or is everything saturated gold?

PASS 3C — THE CAMERA SWEEP
  • Does every shot name an EXACT move from ⑧, with a DIRECTION and
    a SPEED?
  • Count the bold moves — exactly ONE in the whole film.
  • Is any shot stacking two moves? Do two neighbouring shots use
    the same move AND the same size?
  • Does the film keep to 4-6 setups (㉕), each used at least four
    times, and is any scene stuck in one setup?
  • Is the GENRE MODE stated, and does every clip length, move and
    light choice obey it — with the PHASES (㉘) overriding inside
    each phase?
  • Is there exactly one speed ramp, on the peak?
  • Does every shot name ONE practical element, and is it a real
    physical thing rather than a glow or ray?

PASS 3D — MODERN AND BIG
  • Does every character DNA line open with an AGE IN YEARS?
  • Search every prompt for: pores, wrinkles, lines, weathered,
    worn, faded, tarnish, slub, grain, muted, aged, dusty, peeling,
    cracked. On a young character or a present-day setting, every
    one is a defect.
  • Is the capture line modern — digital cinema camera, clean and
    sharp — rather than film stock and grain?
  • Exactly ONE big effect shot, on the peak. Not zero. Not three.
    Is it written as a physical event with its light on her face,
    what it moves on her, what it leaves behind, and her face
    receiving it? Does a reaction shot follow it?
  • Would someone scrolling stop on this? If no single frame is
    worth stopping for, build the peak.

PASS 3E — THE ATMOSPHERE AND FACE SWEEP ⭐
  • Does EVERY effect carry a number — smoke 0-5, fog 0-5, dust
    0-4, rain 0-4, and the flame count for every fire? Any shot
    left saying "some smoke" or "light fog" is a defect.
  • Does any shot with a readable face sit above smoke 2, fog 2 or
    rain 2? Bring it down.
  • Does every face reveal have a direction, a movement and a
    duration, plus one front light source with its distance? Are the
    two shots after a reveal at smoke 0-1?
  • Does every shot start at the exact level the previous shot ended
    on, and does any change of more than one step have a cause?

PASS 3F — THE MECHANICS SWEEP ⭐
  • Does every hold, drape or touch have named hands and the three
    steps (lift → travel → land)? Does contact last a full second,
    with the other person reacting?
  • Does any cloth, hand or garland pass through a body, or change
    shoulder between shots?
  • Does every detail shot have DOES and SETS written?
  • Does every card carry a CLOTH STATE (starts → changes → ends),
    and do neighbouring cards agree?
  • Does every card carry a FRAME INVENTORY with real counts, and is
    everything in the prompts counted there?

PASS 4 — THE REST CHECK
  Any shot that begins or ends at rest is a defect — apart from the
  very first and the very last shot of the film.

PASS 4B — THE SECOND-BY-SECOND AUDIT
  • Count the beat lines. Does the count equal the clip length in
    seconds? Fill them or shorten the clip.
  • Is any second empty, vague, or a repeat of the one before?
  • Does BLOCK 3 state exactly where the body is at the final frame,
    still in motion?
  • Does any beat line contain a SECOND action? Split it.
  • Does the STAYS THE SAME list name the face, hair, every garment
    with its state, every piece of jewellery and what each hand
    holds?
  • Does anything change in the beats that is not in the ONLY THING
    THAT CHANGES list?
  • ⭐ Does the video prompt re-describe her face, clothes or the
    place? Delete those words. That is the "still is the truth"
    law — re-describing is what makes the face change mid-clip.
  • Is more than one camera move asked for? Is the clip longer than
    the action needs?

PASS 4C — THE DEAD SHOT SWEEP
  Does the FRAME change during each clip, or is a person standing in
  one spot while a small part of them moves? Any shot whose only
  movement is a hand, an earring, hair, cloth or a blink, with no
  travel, is a DEAD SHOT — rewrite it. Count the still shots: more
  than one in the film is a defect. Does every card have a MOVEMENT
  THROUGH THE FRAME line, an ENTERS FROM and an EXITS AT? Does the
  travel direction hold across the film? Any two neighbouring camera
  positions less than 30 degrees apart is a jump cut.

PASS 4D — THE TREADMILL SWEEP
  For every travelling card: where is her body at 0 seconds, and
  where at the last second? If it is the same place, rewrite it.
  Does the card state FROM, TO and metres? Does something LEAVE the
  frame behind her, named, with the second it goes? Does something
  GROW ahead? Do the beats name landmarks rather than only body
  parts? Does her size or position change — or, if the camera
  matches her pace, does the background clearly slide past? Does the
  prompt state the camera-to-subject relationship?

PASS 4E — THE PROGRESS SWEEP
  Read only the BEHIND HER column, top to bottom. Does the backdrop
  change at least every two shots? Is the destination visible and
  getting closer? Does the ground underfoot change? Could any two
  shots be swapped without anyone noticing — if yes, rewrite both.
  Do two neighbouring shots share both size and move?

PASS 4F — THE HANDOFF SWEEP
  For every pair: is shot N's last frame the same picture as shot
  N+1's first frame? If the tool takes a last frame, is it said
  explicitly on the card? Does every still work as the previous
  shot's destination — same light direction, same distance class,
  body caught mid-movement? Does any video prompt describe the
  endpoints instead of the journey?

PASS 4G — THE DNA SWEEP
  IMAGE prompts: full DNA line, word for word, every time; never
  shortened to "the same person as before"; full location DNA line.
  VIDEO prompts: SHORT LOCK, not a full re-description — with the
  full line only in the three justified cases. Does every video
  prompt end with the face lock line? Getting this split wrong in
  either direction is a defect.

PASS 4H — THE UPLOAD SWEEP
  Does any card ask me to upload more than one portrait per visible
  character? Does any card ask for a character sheet grid, a world
  sheet or a style image? Remove them from every upload line.
  Does every video card upload the shot still and nothing else,
  except a legitimately pinned last frame? Is the total number of
  images I must make as small as it can be?

PASS 4I — THE LAST FRAME SWEEP
  For every card that pins a last frame: same location? framing
  within one step? subject on the same side? same light? a change a
  real body could make in this clip's length? Any "no" means unpin
  it and write the end state properly instead.

PASS 5 — THE FULL AUDIT
  STORY: every character arrives and leaves on screen · nobody
  appears before their ARRIVES event · no action happens twice ·
  every object tracked · a stranger could never ask "how did they
  get there?"
  CONTINUITY: body state, hands, wardrobe and light checked shot to
  shot · light only moves forward.
  CAMERA: 180-degree line held · screen direction held · eyelines
  match · every angle given an emotional reason · camera height
  stated everywhere.
  FRAMING: every shot states the headcount and rules out duplicates ·
  every shot states the figure size.
  PERFORMANCE: every event has a reaction shot · no two consecutive
  shots hold the same expression · every character's face is
  different at the end than at the beginning.
  CLIP DISCIPLINE: every second booked · constants named · stated
  final frame.
  ROUTE: film travels one way · every place entered and left on
  screen · no place holds more than 3 shots in a row.
  OPENING: opens mid-movement, no warming up.
  THREAD: travelling object in 5+ shots · repeating frame · one
  ambient sound · colour accent.

PASS 6 — FIX
  Rewrite the offending shots. Fix by rewriting the pair — extend
  the action, re-angle, move the cut into the motion, carry the
  camera, carry the sound. Never by inserting filler clips.

PASS 7 — RUN AGAIN
  Go back to PASS 0. Keep looping until a full pass finds ZERO
  defects. Three or four rounds is normal. Do the work.

Then print at the top of your output:
  CORRECTION LOOP: [n] rounds.
  Fixed: [one line each]
  Final pass: 0 defects.
  This film plays as: [one sentence — the single continuous event
  the viewer experiences]

If you cannot honestly write "0 defects", you are not finished.
